- **Trade Capture** — Agents convert unstructured trade instructions into booked, enriched, validated records.
- **Confirmation** — Agents match confirmations against bookings and route exceptions to owners.
- **Clearing & Settlement** — The pilot domain. Agents pre-match instructions, identify alleges, and drive fails and funding decisions.
- **Post-settlement & Asset Servicing** — Agents read corporate-action notices, compute entitlements, and reconcile positions and breaks.
- **Reporting & Control** — Agents enrich, validate and produce regulatory, tax and valuation outputs.
