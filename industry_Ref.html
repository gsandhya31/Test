Consulting-house coverage summary

McKinsey — best coverage: source [9] names operations, client onboarding, settlement & reconciliations, product control and reporting explicitly; [11] and [12] add confirmation, exceptions, custody and clearing. Deloitte — [10] names settlement, collateral management and data reconciliation; Deloitte also facilitated the DTCC/ISC T+1 industry report [7]. Deloitte's capital-markets practice pages list trade capture, matching/confirmation, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, corporate actions, margin/financing and collateral management as its functional scope, but practice/careers pages are not citable in a management deck; use [7] and [10] instead. PwC — no strong publicly available PwC report found that names these operational domains; PwC's relevant publications (e.g. Global Asset & Wealth Management Survey) are AWM-market-sizing oriented rather than ops-taxonomy sources. Recommend not citing PwC for this slide.
